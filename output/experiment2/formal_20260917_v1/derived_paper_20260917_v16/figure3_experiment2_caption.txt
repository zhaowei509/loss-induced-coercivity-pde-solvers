Figure 3. Horizontal positions use $\log_{10}(k+1)$, with ticks labeled by actual accepted Armijo steps $k$. (a) Independent-quadrature solution-error ratio $E(a)/B(a)$ for the cubic semilinear problem: line, median; band, full range over 10 fixed random-feature seeds; dashed line, theoretical upper bound 1. (b) Independent 256-point Ritz stationarity residual $\rho_4$: blue line and band, median and IQR over 10 unscreened random-feature trajectories in original coordinates; orange line, one fixed constructed-basis trajectory. Both are shown at their actual accepted steps through 50,000. The green main-panel line connects the QR-coordinate medians at the two common early checkpoints ($k=0,50$). The arrow leads to an inset connecting each of the 10 QR runs' saved independent-evaluation checkpoints through its actual stop (65-543 steps); no post-stop values are drawn. All QR runs stopped by numerical step underflow, not by the gradient threshold. QR changes the gradient dynamics, so this is not a same-dynamics rate comparison.
